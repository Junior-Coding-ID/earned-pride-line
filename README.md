## The entry

One line, 157 characters — a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene:

> Dad had spent a winter practicing his name on the backs of bills; when the parcel came, he signed for it himself and held the little screen up for me to see.

A father who spent a whole winter practicing his signature on the backs of bills finally signs for a parcel himself — a small person doing something hard, a long effort finally landing.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT/entry.txt` when set, for local testing). It creates the output directory first, then writes the entry as UTF-8 text.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
